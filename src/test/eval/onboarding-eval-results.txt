Every run was on fallback models: the onboarding chain's first model had no

gemini-3.1-flash-lite and cohere command-a-plus under per-minute limits. None of

The runs below are the history of the design. Each fix moved a rule the models
were ignoring out of the instruction and into code.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RUN 1 — instruction only
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5/6 scenarios fully passed, 44/45 checks

The checks graded the end state, which hid the real failure: in 4 of 6
onboardings the model read back and closed after seven or eight answers, with
long-term goals, habits and behaviour never asked. Only college-first-time
failed a check (behaviour empty — "just be straight with me" arrived after the
close).

  FIX: completeFlow refuses "done" while any notes section is empty
       (unfinishedSections), and ONBOARDING STATE says READY / NOT READY TO
       FINISH. The eval records whether onboarding was still open after each
       reply, so an early close is visible.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RUN 2 — the finish gate, with a hint in its refusal
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5/6 scenarios fully passed, 53/54 checks

The refusal said: 'If they would rather not say, save "Prefers not to say."'.
Models took it as the way past the gate. In 4 of 6 onboardings they wrote the
skip into sections they had never asked about — up to four at once — and
closed. school-first-time closed before "my half-yearly exams are next month"
arrived, leaving shortTermGoals as "Prefers not to say.".

  FIX: the hint was removed. Declines were read from the person's own words by
       code (classifyReply) and counted on the flow; the gate refused more
       skips than declines.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RUN 3 — declines counted at the close
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5/7 scenarios fully passed, 64/66 checks (skips-one-topic added)

  FAIL  review-after-move  about kept "Backend developer in Pune" and appended
                           "Moved to Bengaluru" — two cities, one wrong
  FAIL  skips-one-topic    two questions flagged; both were one topic ("working
                           hours, and when do you wake") — a false positive in
                           the eval's check, since fixed

What the checks missed, read from the transcripts:
  - skips-one-topic: one "I'd rather not say" was written into four sections.
    The gate held, but the count let the onboarding close with behaviour still
    a skip after they said "be gentle" — a "yes" after the close repaired it,
    and the end-state check passed.
  - abandon-resume: one reply reached the user as
    "opaquecall:default_api:loadSkill{skill:userContextEnrichment}" — a tool
    call written as text. No check looked for it.

  FIX: REVIEW MODE says to rewrite a section with only what is true now.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RUN 4 — the two failing scenarios re-run
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
review-after-move  10/10 — about now reads "... living in Bengaluru", no Pune
skips-one-topic     6/6 — and still wrong. Behaviour ended as "Prefers not to
                    say." although they said "be gentle":
  - a skip was written into Behaviour in a turn where they answered about times
  - completeFlow ran in the same step as the note writes and read the notes
    before they landed; its refusal named Behaviour as a skip they never made
  - the model then wrote the skip over "Prefers a gentle approach.", which it
    had just saved

  FIX: createSkipGuard checks the write instead of counting at the close — a
       skip only in a turn whose message turned something down, one section
       per decline, never over an answer. runStepCalls starts completeFlow
       after the step's other calls. completeFlow judges nothing when no
       onboarding is open. The eval grades the notes as they stood when
       onboarding closed, and every scenario checks for invented skips and
       for tool calls leaked as text.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RUN 5 — the skip guard, completeFlow last, graded at the close
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6/7 scenarios fully passed, 73/75 checks

  pass  college-first-time 20/20, school-first-time 13/13, everything-at-once
        9/9, review-after-move 11/11, declines-routines 8/8, abandon-resume 5/5
  FAIL  skips-one-topic 7/9 — habits and behaviour were "None." when onboarding

  FIX: saysNothing — the guard covers any note that says nothing ("None.",

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RUN 6 — skips-one-topic twice, after the filler fix
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
run 1  7/9   run 2  9/9

Both runs did what the design is for. Five times across the two, the model
tried "Prefers not to say." for habits or behaviour in a turn where they had
answered something else; each was refused with "Ask about Habits instead", and
completeFlow — now after the step's writes — refused while behaviour was empty.
Both onboardings closed on "be gentle", with every section holding the
person's own answer.

Run 1's two failures were the eval's own:
  - /gentle/ does not match "Prefers to be pushed gently..."
  - "What does your work at the pharmacy usually involve, and what is your team
    like there?" was flagged as two topics; work and team are one
Both checks fixed.

RUN 7 — full run, stopped by a session restart after four scenarios
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
college-first-time 20/20, school-first-time 8/13, everything-at-once 9/9,
review-after-move 11/11 — then the process was stopped. By now both gemini-3.5
models were out of quota, and turns ran on gemini-3.1-flash-lite.

  FAIL  school-first-time 8/13 — onboarding was closed as SKIPPED halfway
        through. The model had asked about favourite subjects; "those times
        are fine" and "I don't eat eggs" did not answer that, so it counted
        both as unrelated replies — while saving both, to settings and About.
        On the next reply it saved the exams as a goal and closed onboarding
        as skipped in the same step. The rest went in as ordinary
        conversation, onboardedAt stayed null, and routines never started.

  FIX: IF THEY SAY SOMETHING ELSE says anything about themselves is an answer,
       even when it is not what was asked. createSkipGuard now hears what each
       call did: a turn that saved a note or a setting cannot close onboarding
       as skipped.
